# Tacurong City

First-class municipality and commercial center of Sultan Kudarat province, Philippines. Location of [[villon-farm-supply|Villon Farm Supply]] per the POS and Purchasing module papers.

## Relevance to VISTA

- Active agricultural trading community
- Significant farm supply business presence
- Sunday is local market day → highest transaction volume
- Palay planting/growing season drives demand spikes
