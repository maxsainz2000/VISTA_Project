# Feedback and Support

## In-product feedback

1. Click **feedback** in the navigation bar
2. Pick 👍 or 👎
3. Fill in the short form and submit

It goes straight to the Google Labs team. The Jules product and engineering team reviews submissions regularly to prioritise fixes, spot workflow friction, and find corner cases. They don't reply individually but do read everything.

## Good feature requests say

- what you were trying to do
- what got in the way
- how you imagine it working

Concrete real-world examples carry the most weight.
